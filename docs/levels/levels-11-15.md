# Levels 11-15

Zone 3 **Mansion** continues (L11-12). Zone 4 **Haunted Manor** (L13-16): darker palette, flashlights, spooky but cartoonish (ghost-sheet decor, no horror).

---

## L11 "Grandma's Study"
- **Teaches:** 3-digit safe codes assembled from several clues, 4th inventory slot unlocked.
- **Layout:** 2 floors, 38x20 each. Study, music room, master bedroom, balcony. Home Base on the balcony.
- **Time:** 180 s (par 110).
- **Seekers:** Grandma Gus (study), Marlo (music room, checks the piano), Sprinter Sibling (stairs loop).
- **Locked doors:** master bedroom (Gold), balcony door (code lock).
- **Clues:** digit 1 = number of candles on a painting, digit 2 = the hour a clock is stopped at, digit 3 = the page number on a note. The notebook shows all found clues.
- **Items:** Gold Key in the trophy case, Potion x2, Trophy in the music box.
- **Intended solution:** gather digits floor 1, open the safe, take the key, go upstairs, cross the music room while the sprinter is on the opposite floor.

---

## L12 "The Switch Room"
- **Teaches:** switch timing and fuse box.
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 40x24. Central hub with four wings. Each wing has a switch. Home Base opens only when two switches are active at once (10 s window).
- **Time:** 170 s (par 105).
- **Seekers:** Four patrols, one per wing, each with a different pattern. Auntie Alarm not yet.
- **Mechanic:** the fuse box turns lights off on this floor for 20 s (halves detection, but Pip's flashlight is dim too).
- **Clues:** the order of switches is painted on the wallpaper.
- **Items:** Flashlight, Stopwatch x2, Sticky Gum, Trophy on the chandelier cord (reach via a bookshelf climb).
- **Intended solution:** flip the nearest switch, travel to the opposite wing during the dark window, flip the second within 10 s.

## L13 "Candlelit Corridors" (Zone 4 intro)
- **Teaches:** the Night Watcher with a flashlight beam.
- **Layout:** 2 floors, 38x22 + basement. Dark. Only candles and the watcher's flashlight illuminate.
- **Time:** 180 s (par 115).
- **Seekers:** **Night Watcher** (narrow beam, swings left/right), Marlo, and a **Ghost-sheet prank** (looks like a seeker but is harmless, tells the player by gently bobbing).
- **Locked doors:** cellar (Silver), crypt-like vault (Gold).
- **Clues:** the portrait's eyes point to the right painting (look direction). Letter on a pillow: "The cellar key sleeps in a boot."
- **Items:** Mirror Shard, Potion, Slippers, Trophy in the vault.
- **Intended solution:** use the beam's blind spot behind the watcher.

---

## L14 "Door Guard"
- **Teaches:** stationary guard and distractions.
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 36x20. A guard blocks the only door to Home Base. The guard leaves when distracted (radio, squeaky toy, decoy teddy).
- **Time:** 160 s (par 100).
- **Seekers:** **The Door Guard** (stationary), Grandma Gus, Biscuit the dog.
- **Locked doors:** the toy room (Bronze), the nursery (Silver) with the teddy.
- **Clues:** the teddy is hidden in the nursery wardrobe, the radio is in the library, the squeaky toy in the toy chest. The player needs 2 of the 3 to move the guard (the guard needs both a radio and a lure time of 6 s).
- **Items:** Treat for Biscuit, Slippers, Potion.
- **Intended solution:** place the radio in the kitchen to pull the guard, then slip past with slippers while Biscuit chews a treat.

---

## L15 "Mirror Twins"
- **Teaches:** mirrored patrol routes and reading patterns.
- **Layout:** 2 floors that mirror each other, 32x20. Floor 1 and floor 2 are identical in geometry, with different items.
- **Time:** 170 s (par 110).
- **Seekers:** Mirror Twin A (floor 1), Mirror Twin B (floor 2) mirrors A's path. Using stairs puts Pip on the same relative spot at the opposite phase.
- **Locked doors:** Gold gates on both floors. Key halves: one on each floor.
- **Clues:** two paintings that look the same but have one difference each. Spotting the difference reveals the Key Half's hiding place.
- **Items:** Potion x2, Stopwatch, Trophy behind the staircase.
- **Intended solution:** use stairs to dodge a twin by switching floors at the right moment.
