# Levels 1-5

Zone 1 **Cozy Cottage** (L1-4): tutorial, small, forgiving. Zone 2 **Suburban House** starts at L5.
Grid sizes are in tiles. Times are seconds. "Safe window" = guaranteed gap at the main chokepoint.

Legend: S = start, H = Home Base, K = key, D = door, # = wall.

---

## L1 "First Peek"
- **Goal of the level:** teach move, sneak, cones, exit. No items needed.
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 24x14. Living room (start) -> hallway -> kitchen -> Home Base door on the east wall.
- **Time:** 90 s (par 45 s).
- **Seekers:** Grandma Gus patrols the hallway (up/down, 4 s pauses at each end). Uncle Marlo stands in the kitchen and turns left/right every 3 s.
- **Locked doors:** none.
- **Clues/items:** Trophy (teddy bear on the bookshelf, 1.0 s search). One Stopwatch (+10 s) in the fridge.
- **Searchables:** bookshelf, sofa cushion (coin), fridge, kitchen drawer.
- **Teaching prompts (3 only):** "Hold to sneak", "Stay out of the yellow", "Reach the green door".
- **Intended solution:** wait for Grandma to walk to the back of the hall, slip behind the sofa, cross when she faces away, hug the south kitchen wall while Marlo faces north, exit.
- **Fail forgiveness:** seekers have 25% longer pauses (assist).

## L2 "Behind the Couch"
- **Teaches:** hiding spots and noise rings.
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 26x14. Bedroom (start) -> hallway -> living room -> Home Base on the north side. Doors are closed (creak noise unless sneaking).
- **Time:** 100 s (par 55).
- **Seekers:** Grandma Gus loops hallway and living room (clockwise). Twin pair not yet. One extra Grandma in the living room, asleep (cone off) for 6 s every 12 s.
- **Locked doors:** none.
- **Items:** Trophy under the rug in the living room (0.8 s). Radio on the bookshelf (Booster intro: place it to pull Grandma away).
- **Searchables:** wardrobe (hide, safe), nightstand (potion tutorial? no, empty), under-bed (hide), toy box (squeaky toy trap), rug.
- **New prompts:** "Hide in the wardrobe", "Running is loud", "Use the radio to lure Grandma".
- **Intended solution:** run is tempting but loud. Wait inside the wardrobe until Grandma passes, sneak along the hall, use the radio to pull the second Grandma off the north door, exit.

## L3 "The Pantry Key"
- **Teaches:** keys, locked doors, searching with a risk.
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 28x16. Start in the porch, kitchen in the middle, a **locked pantry (Bronze)** blocks the route to Home Base (the pantry leads to the back garden gate).
- **Time:** 110 s (par 60).
- **Seekers:** Grandma Gus (kitchen loop, 5 s), **Uncle Marlo (Snoopy intro)** checks the coat closet and the wardrobe on his route.
- **Locked doors:** pantry (Bronze).
- **Clue chain:** a painting of a flower pot in the hall -> Bronze Key under the plant pot on the porch (2 tiles from start). Marlo's route makes the porch safe only on the first 10 s.
- **Items:** Trophy in the coat closet (risky, Marlo checks it). Stopwatch in the pantry.
- **Intended solution:** grab the key early (porch pot), then plan a path past Marlo to the pantry.
- **Teaching prompts:** "A key! Doors that match open.", "Marlo checks closets."

## L4 "Quiet Feet"
- **Teaches:** surfaces and noise (carpet, tile, creaky boards), slippers.
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 30x16. Long hall with creaky boards, a kitchen with tile, a carpeted nursery.
- **Time:** 120 s (par 70).
- **Seekers:** Grandma Gus (long patrol), Marlo (checks closets), a **baby monitor** object (a stationary noise sensor: creates a noise event at Grandma when Pip is within 2.5 tiles while running).
- **Locked doors:** nursery door (Bronze).
- **Clues:** Drawer note: "Soft steps keep secrets." points to Sneaky Slippers in the shoe rack.
- **Items:** Bronze Key inside the piano bench, Slippers (20 s) in the shoe rack, Trophy in the toy chest (squeaky trap 30%).
- **Intended solution:** slippers let Pip run across the creaky hall with no noise.

## L5 "Garage Night" (Zone 2 intro)
- **Teaches:** two sections, the first **potion** pickup, and a one-way door.
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 34x18. Front yard -> house (section A) -> garage (section B) -> back door = Home Base. A **Silver** door separates A from B.
- **Time:** 130 s (par 75).
- **Seekers:** Grandma Gus (section A), Marlo (checks garage shelves), **Tilly and Tobi** not yet. A **sleeping dog**, harmless but wakes if Pip runs within 4 tiles (intro to Biscuit).
- **Locked doors:** garage (Silver).
- **Clues:** Painting hides a wall safe (3 digits). Digits are on a photo, a calendar, and a clock. Safe holds the Silver Key. (A simpler variant: key hidden behind the painting. Use the safe variant from this level on.)
- **Items:** **Invisibility Potion** in the fridge (first one). Stopwatch +15 in the car glovebox. Trophy under the car's seat mat.
- **Intended solution:** read clues to open the safe, take the key, drink the potion for the last corridor where Marlo and Grandma overlap.
