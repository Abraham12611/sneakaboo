# Levels 16-20

> **Layout rule (Nov 25):** map sizes below are footprints including the yard. Every level uses a different real-house floor plan with several compact rooms, see `docs/17-floor-plan-and-layout-principles.md` (archetype per level in its mapping table). Read any 'big room' as a set of partitioned rooms.


Zone 4 **Haunted Manor** ends at L16. Zone 5 **Grand Estate** (L17-20): museum wing, castle tower, final showdown. Highest difficulty: still fair, longer, more routes.

---

## L16 "Clockwork Hall"
- **Teaches:** timed cones (clock-driven sweeps).
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 36x20. A big clock room with 3 **Cuckoo Clock Granddads** whose cones sweep on timers (0 to 180 degrees over 6 s). Chimes every 30 s create a noise event.
- **Time:** 160 s (par 100).
- **Seekers:** 3 granddads (stationary sweepers), Sprinter Sibling doing laps.
- **Locked doors:** Gear door. Gears A, B, C are inside three different clocks (each clock's pendulum opens).
- **Clues:** the hour on each clock face is in the "chime order".
- **Items:** Stopwatch x3 (hidden in the clocks), Potion, Slippers.
- **Intended solution:** read each sweep, cross on the beat.

## L17 "Museum After Hours" (Zone 5 intro)
- **Teaches:** alarm seeker (Auntie Alarm) and glass cases.
- **Layout:** 2 floors, 44x24 floor 1, 40x22 floor 2. Exhibit halls, a sculpture garden.
- **Time:** 200 s (par 125).
- **Seekers:** **Auntie Alarm** (raises an alarm if she sees Pip even half way: all seekers rush to her spot for 8 s, giving a chance to sneak elsewhere), Marlo, twins, Biscuit.
- **Locked doors:** Silver (east wing), Gold (exit), a glass case with a Key Half.
- **Clues:** a museum map on the wall highlights the "closed" exhibits. The player must read the plaque riddles.
- **Items:** Whistle (first one), Potion x2, Map Scroll, Trophy inside a glass case (needs a Bronze Key).
- **Intended solution:** deliberately trigger the alarm to draw seekers away from the east wing, then slip through.

## L18 "Tower Climb"
- **Teaches:** vertical layout, many floors.
- **Layout:** 4 small floors (24x16 each), connected by stairs and a dumbwaiter. Home Base at the top.
- **Time:** 210 s (par 130).
- **Seekers:** one per floor, each with different behavior: floor 1 patrol, floor 2 Snoopy, floor 3 sprinter, floor 4 door guard.
- **Locked doors:** one key per floor, each hidden on the floor below (drag keys upward). 3 keys total, the 4th floor is open.
- **Clues:** letters on each floor describe the next floor's key place.
- **Items:** Slippers, Potion x2, Stopwatch x2, Sticky Gum.
- **Shortcut:** the dumbwaiter takes Pip one floor up for 2 s of noise.

---

## L19 "Library of Secrets"
- **Teaches:** puzzle-heavy route with secret passages, minimal combat-like pressure.
- **Layout:** 2 floors, 36x22. A huge library with secret doors behind bookcases.
- **Time:** 220 s (par 140).
- **Seekers:** Grandma Gus (reading chair, sleepy), Marlo (checks hidden spots), a **Page Turner** seeker who closes secret doors behind Pip, and Biscuit.
- **Locked doors:** secret bookcases (open with the right book order), Silver and Gold doors, a safe with a 4-digit code.
- **Clues:** book spines show numbers. A poem gives the order. Painting shows a candle-count digit.
- **Items:** Mirror Shard, Map Scroll x2, Potion, Whistle.
- **Intended solution:** secret passages bypass most seekers if the clue order is solved in one go.

## L20 "Home Base" (Finale)
- **Teaches:** nothing new. Uses everything.
- **Layout:** 3 floors + garden, 56x30 floor 1. The whole estate: kitchen, library, gallery, clock room, tower. Home Base is the **garden gate** guarded by the Head Seeker.
- **Time:** 300 s (par 190).
- **Seekers:** the whole family: Grandma Gus, Marlo, twins, Biscuit, Sprinter, Auntie Alarm, Night Watcher. **Head Seeker** (boss behavior): remembers hiding spots Pip used and checks them.
- **Locked doors:** 3 Gold doors, 2 Silver, 1 Bronze, 1 gear machine, 1 safe. Stages must be solved in 3 sections.
- **Clues:** the final riddle assembles hints from earlier levels (callbacks).
- **Items:** 4 potions, 2 slippers, 3 stopwatches, a Skeleton Key hidden for 3-star runs, and the **Golden Trophy**.
- **Three-phase structure:**
  1. Ground floor: gather keys, choose a route.
  2. Upper floors: solve the code and take the Gold Key.
  3. Garden dash: the Head Seeker is released when Pip touches the gate chain. 15 seconds of chase (Tag Chase rules) to the gate.
- **Reward:** ending cutscene, "Found you... at last!" Grandma smiles and hugs Pip. Unlocks Hard Mode (+20% seeker speed, -15% time) and the level builder for stars.
