# Levels 6-10

Zone 2 **Suburban House** (L5-8), Zone 3 **Mansion** begins at L9.

---

## L6 "Double Trouble"
- **Teaches:** twins (two cones that overlap), timing windows.
- **Layout:** 1 floor + attic crawl space, 36x18. Living room, dining room, bathroom, backyard patio with Home Base at the shed.
- **Time:** 140 s (par 80).
- **Seekers:** Tilly and Tobi walk in a pair through the dining room. Grandma Gus covers the hall. When one twin is Curious, the other turns too.
- **Locked doors:** back patio (Bronze).
- **Clues:** Bathroom medicine cabinet note: "Look where the plants drink." Key in the watering can on the patio steps (reachable only before the door).
- **Items:** Potion in the bathroom cabinet, Trophy in the dining room sideboard, Squeaky Toy in a toy box.
- **Shortcut:** vent in the bathroom to the patio (screwdriver in the junk drawer).
- **Intended solution:** the twins' window is 4 s. Use the squeaky toy to pull them to the living room, then cross.

---

## L7 "Upstairs, Downstairs"
- **Teaches:** two floors and stairs.
- **Layout:** 2 floors, 30x16 each. Start on the ground floor. Home Base is the **rooftop hatch** (upstairs, back bedroom).
- **Time:** 150 s (par 85).
- **Seekers:** Floor 1: Grandma Gus. Floor 2: Marlo checking bedrooms. A seeker on the stairs every 20 s alternates floors.
- **Locked doors:** upstairs bedroom (Silver).
- **Clues:** Floor 1 hall painting shows 3 colored dots (red, blue, yellow) -> a keypad lock on the stairs gate needs "red, blue, yellow" tap order. The pad is a simple 3-button mini panel.
- **Items:** Silver Key in the attic suitcase (floor 2). Stopwatch in the kitchen clock. Trophy in the laundry basket.
- **Intended solution:** go up, find the key, avoid Marlo's bedroom checks by hiding in the wardrobe at the right moment.

## L8 "Midnight Snack"
- **Teaches:** darkness, light switches, and Biscuit the dog.
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 34x18, dark. Only lamps and the fridge light the floor. Flashlight in the nightstand.
- **Time:** 140 s (par 80).
- **Seekers:** **Biscuit the dog** (short cone, long hearing) loops the kitchen. Grandma Gus sleeps on the sofa and wakes if loud.
- **Locked doors:** none, but the pantry door is **jammed** and needs Key Half A + B.
- **Clues:** Key Half A in a boot, Key Half B in the umbrella stand. Hint: a post-it "Both halves, split in two shoes."
- **Items:** Slippers in the closet (counter to Biscuit's hearing), Treat in the cookie jar (distracts Biscuit), Trophy under the dog bed.
- **Intended solution:** slippers + treat to pass the dog quietly.

## L9 "Welcome to the Manor" (Zone 3 intro)
- **Teaches:** the sprinter, a bigger house, three key colors.
- **Layout:** 2 floors, 40x22 floor 1, 36x20 floor 2. Grand hall, library, kitchen, study. Home Base is the front garden gazebo (outside, via the Gold door).
- **Time:** 170 s (par 100).
- **Seekers:** **Sprinter Sibling** loops the hall (fast, narrow). Grandma Gus in the library, Marlo in the study.
- **Locked doors:** library (Bronze), study (Silver), exit (Gold).
- **Clue chain:** Bronze key (hall plant) -> library -> Silver key hidden behind the 3rd book -> study -> Gold key inside the globe.
- **Items:** Potion in the study desk, Stopwatch in the grandfather clock, Trophy on the high shelf (needs the ladder; moving it makes noise).
- **Chain design note:** each step opens an area with a safe corner so the player can recover.

---

## L10 "The Long Gallery"
- **Teaches:** patterns and timing, seeker alerts.
- **Layout:** 1 floor, 46x14. A long museum-like gallery with paintings. Home Base at the far end.
- **Time:** 160 s (par 100).
- **Seekers:** Four seekers in staggered patrols along the gallery (Grandma, Marlo, twins). Alerts are shared: if one sees Pip, the others go Investigate.
- **Locked doors:** two Silver doors (side rooms) hold the Gear A and Gear B. Gear C is on a wall painting behind a mirror. The drawbridge (final gate) needs all 3 gears.
- **Clues:** Painting riddle: "I show you yourself, but I'm not you." -> mirror at the end of the hall.
- **Items:** Slippers, Potion x2, Map Scroll (shows patrols for 5 s).
- **Mid-boss feel:** after taking all 3 gears the drawbridge lowers (loud noise). Seekers move toward it. Pip must reach Home Base before they arrive.
- **Intended solution:** use the map scroll to chart the gaps, use slippers on the creaky center, grab gears in order, run at the end when the noise is unavoidable.
