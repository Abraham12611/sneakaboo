# 12 Economy and Meta

Contest build: free, no purchases, no accounts. The meta layer is **local** and cosmetic. Anything with money comes after the contest and after store launch design.

## Currency
- **Coins:** earned per level (base 5 + 2 per star + 1 per coin pickup). Spent on hints and cosmetics.
- **Toy Tokens:** found in secrets. Unlock outfits only.
- **Golden Acorns:** 3 per zone. Unlock zone cosmetics and the bonus comic.

## Spending
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Hint (highlights target 3 s) | 10 coins |
| Show seeker paths (5 s, once per attempt) | 15 coins |
| Extra 10 s on the timer (before level start, once) | 20 coins |
| Starting booster (potion) | 30 coins |
| Pip outfits (hat, cape, pajamas, ninja, pirate) | 40 to 120 coins |
| Seeker silly hats (cosmetic) | 20 to 60 coins |

Rule: **never pay-to-win in the story.** Hints and boosters make levels easier, not required. 3 stars must be earnable with no spent items.

## Progression
- Linear unlock: finish a level to unlock the next. Zones unlock at L5, L9, L13, L17.
- Star gates: L11 needs 20 stars total, L17 needs 40 stars. Total stars: 60. Prevents rushing and encourages replay.
- Mastery: Hard Mode unlocks after L20.

## Daily and retention (post-contest)
- Daily Challenge: seeded level, same for everyone, one reward chest.
- Streak: consecutive days give coins.
- Weekly Build Jam: level builder theme, featured winner.

## Achievements (examples)
| Name | Condition |
|------|-----------|
| Ghost | Finish a level with no seeker above 0.4. |
| Lead Foot | Win a level using run only 5 times. |
| Pack Rat | Collect every trophy in a zone. |
| Hide Master | Hide in 10 spots in one level. |
| Speed Sneaker | Finish L1-5 under par. |
| Locksmith | Open every locked door type. |
| Friendly Tag | Get found by Grandma 10 times (comedy badge). |

## Monetization plan (after store launch)
Options, compared:
| Model | Pros | Cons |
|-------|------|------|
| Premium (paid once) | Clean experience, simple | Lower installs |
| Free + rewarded ads (extra time, hint) | Fits the core loop, optional | Needs ad SDK, parental compliance |
| Free + cosmetics IAP | No gameplay impact | Needs art volume |
| Level pack IAP | Fits the story | Needs new levels |

Recommended: free with rewarded ads (opt-in) + cosmetic IAP + a one-time "Remove ads" purchase. Child-directed apps need COPPA/Families policy compliance (no behavioral ads), see doc 13.
