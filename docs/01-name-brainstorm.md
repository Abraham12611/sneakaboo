# 01 Name Brainstorm

Rules: original, short, easy to say, works as an app-store title, no clash with known game titles. Verify trademark and store search before the store launch (not done yet).

## Shortlist
| Rank | Name | Why | Risk |
|------|------|-----|------|
| 1 | **Sneakaboo** | Sneak + peekaboo. Instantly says "stealth hide and seek". Cute and memorable. | Check for existing apps with the same name. |
| 2 | **Hush House** | Calm, mysterious, fits the house setting. Strong logo shape. | Common phrase, several small apps use it. |
| 3 | **Tiptoe Tag** | Describes the verbs. Friendly for kids. | Sounds younger than the target. |
| 4 | **Creep & Seek** | Pun on hide and seek, hints at stealth. | "Creep" may read as edgy for stores. |
| 5 | **Pip Sneaks Out** | Names the hero, supports a series and merch. | Less clear about the genre. |

## More ideas
**Wordplay:** Hide & Sneak, Sneak Peek Manor, Peekaboo Manor, Seek No More, Seekers Keepers, Found You!, Marco Slow, Shh! Mansion, Creep Keep, Pipsqueak Prowl, Tiptoe Manor, Quiet Quarters, Ghost in the Hallway.

**Verb-focused:** Slink, Slip Away, Sneak Out, Duck & Dodge, Hush Rush, Stealthy Steps, Last Door, Home Base Dash.

**Theme-focused:** Family Reunion Sneak, Grandma's Mansion, Midnight Hide-and-Seek, Lights Out Tag.

**Level-builder angle (for later):** Sneakaboo Workshop, Hide It Yourself, Make-a-Manor.

## Tagline options
- "Don't get found."
- "Hide. Sneak. Home base."
- "The whole house is looking for you."
- "Shhh... they're counting."

## Recommended
**SNEAKABOO** with tagline "The whole house is looking for you."
Hero: **Pip**. Seekers: Grandma Gus, Uncle Marlo, Twins Tilly and Tobi, Biscuit the dog, and the Night Watcher.

## Naming checks to do before store launch
1. Search App Store and Google Play for exact name and near matches.
2. Search USPTO/EUIPO trademark databases for "Sneakaboo".
3. Reserve handles and a domain (sneakaboo.game, .app).
